Why it isn't 100%

U/I won't claim a message translated completely when it didn't. Some things genuinely don't have a clean equivalent for a different kind of reader — U/I will say so in a note rather than quietly smoothing over what got lost.

Your passcode, voice notes, and any archive-derived messages are stored only in this browser, on this device. Voice notes and archive-derived messages never leave it; a message you translate, along with your passcode, goes to the app owner's proxy on its way to Anthropic. There is no account, no export — the "clear all local data" button really deletes what's here.
